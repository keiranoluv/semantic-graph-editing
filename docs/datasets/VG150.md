## Notes

- Do not commit downloaded data.
- Geometry-only historical runs do not require JPEG. The new frozen CLIP
  region-feature experiment downloads all split JPEGs using
  `python -m scripts.download_vg150_images`; see
  [level1_clip_regions.md](../experiments/level1_clip_regions.md).
- Never randomly resplit the benchmark.
- Do not mix dataset variants without verifying compatibility.
- The manifest generator does not overwrite existing outputs.

## Bounding Box Preprocessing

The VG150 annotation loader supports two bounding-box policies:

- `strict` (default): Clip minor coordinate rounding overflow only
  (up to `0.5 * max(width, height) / 1024` pixels). Reject larger
  boundary violations.
- `clip`: Clamp bounding boxes to image boundaries. Reject boxes with
  non-finite coordinates, invalid geometry, or zero area after clipping.

Example:

    loader = VG150AnnotationLoader(
        root=Path("data/raw/vg150"),
        split="train",
        split_manifest=Path("data/raw/vg150/splits.json"),
        box_policy="clip",
    )

The original HDF5 annotations are never modified.

The `clip` policy is a project-specific preprocessing choice.
It must not be assumed equivalent to the official VG150 benchmark
preprocessing without further verification.

For reproducibility, all experiments must record their `box_policy`.
Training, validation, and testing must use a consistent policy.
